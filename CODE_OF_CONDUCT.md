# Code of conduct

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, issue and pull-request discussions,
reviews, and private project communications. Everyone who participates is
expected to help keep those spaces professional, inclusive, and focused on the
work.

## Unacceptable conduct

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or target another person. Do
not publish private information, impersonate others, disrupt project work,
submit malicious content, or use abusive, sexual, or hateful language. Do not
misuse credentials, permissions, or project resources.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through a private
reporting channel rather than a public issue. Maintainers may remove content,
limit participation, request corrective action, or end participation when
needed to protect the project community. They will handle reports as
confidentially as practical and will avoid retaliation against good-faith
reports.
